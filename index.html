THE DUST BOWL: IN DEPTH

The Dust Bowl:(1930-1940), occured across 100 million acres that centered on the Texas Panhandle and Oklahoma Panhandle and touched adjacent sections of New Mexico, Colorado, and Kansas.
The reigion regularly refered to as the great plains, an area that recives little rain water and prone to droughts.

The BACKSTORY:

The reason im foucasing on this topic is due my facsination about the dust storms and how they were caused by excsessive human farming and lack of knowlage about the landscape. The treeless, barren landscape, home to flat airy grasslands became the perfect place for wheat production during the late 1800s.
"Passed on May 20, 1862, the Homestead Act accelerated the settlement of the western territory by granting adult heads of families 160 acres of surveyed public land for a minimal filing fee and five years of continuous residence on that land." As demand for wheat production grew, more and more land was dedicated soley to farming, which destroyed the natural landscape. And after the great depression hit, and prices for wheat plummet, farmers began to tear up even more land in desperation. The soil was not held down by the roots of the native grass. that combined with series of severe droughts and brutal winds led to dust storms......
